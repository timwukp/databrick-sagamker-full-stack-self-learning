Databricks × AWS, one use case end to end

One of the fastest ways to learn Databricks on your own: take a realistic retail scenario from raw store POS data all the way to inventory and sales forecasts, then do the same thing on AWS (SageMaker and related services) and compare cell by cell. Every step has a 3D interactive diagram, Before / After synthetic data, schemas and links to official docs.

DatabricksAWS

The use case

A retailer with thousands of stores in Southeast Asia wants to reduce both stockouts and excess inventory. This is really two forecasts, running on two different clocks.

Warehouse inventory forecastStore sales forecast
GrainWarehouse × SKU × dayStore × SKU × day
Horizon and frequencyNext 30 days, nightly batchRest of today, intraday in real time
OutputSuggested purchase order, sent back to ERP for approvalAn action for the store manager: “Restock 60 from the back room before noon”
ServingBatch inference written to a tablereal-time endpoint

The world in the synthetic data

Three stores (Singapore SG-ORCH-001, Kuala Lumpur MY-KUL-014, Bangkok TH-BKK-007), two warehouses (DC-SG-01, DC-TH-02), three SKUs, business date 2026-10-09 (Friday), with SKU-20871 at 20% off in SG and MY. The raw data deliberately contains duplicate events, late events, a new column, bad records, nested items, local time strings and zero-padded SAP material numbers; each step handles part of them. All numbers reconcile from start to finish; for example, the final order suggestion 1104 = 46 cases × 24.
